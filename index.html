Training information

Start where you are.

Do I need any flight experience?

No. The $299 introductory lesson is designed as a legitimate first instructional flight and requires no prior aviation knowledge.

Can I continue after the introductory flight?

Yes. If you want to pursue a certificate, the next step is a training plan based on your goals, schedule, aircraft access, and applicable aeronautical-experience requirements.

Can you help if I already started somewhere else?

Yes. Finish-up training begins with a logbook and proficiency review so the plan reflects what you actually need rather than restarting a syllabus.

Where does training take place?

Phoenix, Arizona, primarily from Falcon Field (KFFZ) and Deer Valley (KDVT). Other locations are available on request.
